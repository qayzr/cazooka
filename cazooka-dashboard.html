Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Have questions about Trudax? We've compiled answers to the most common questions from enterprise clients to help you make an informed decision.

What is Trudax? +
Trudax is an AI agent that allows you to get immediate answers from all your dashboard data just by asking questions in your language. Instead of manually clicking through multiple complex dashboards, you can simply ask, "Are we on track to meet our financial projections?" and get a single, consolidated answer.
How is this different from just using PowerBI / Dashboards? +
Trudax delivers speed and simplicity. Getting answers from PowerBI/Dashboards often requires analysts to manually hunt through multiple dashboards and compile reports, which can take days. Trudax queries all relevant dashboards at once and gives you a structured answer in minutes.
Is my data secure with Trudax? +
Yes. Security is foundational to our platform. Your data never leaves your organization's environment. Trudax automatically inherits your existing PowerBI permissions, so users can only access data they already have permission to view. All chat history is masked, and a full audit log is available for compliance.
Can Trudax pull data from multiple dashboards at once? +
Yes. This is a core feature. You can ask a single question, and our AI agents will pull and consolidate answers from multiple different PowerBI dashboards and projects, giving you a complete answer without you needing to check each dashboard individually.
Is Trudax accessible on mobile devices? +
Yes. The Trudax web application is fully mobile-responsive for on-the-go access. We also offer a premium service to access Trudax directly within WhatsApp for immediate insights, anytime and anywhere.
What data sources does Trudax support? +
Trudax integrates directly with your existing PowerBI dashboards. Support for other major platforms like Tableau, Qlik Sense, and direct Excel integration is on our future roadmap.
